# Plate Coach

An evidence-based gym coach that runs in the browser and installs to your home screen.

- Builds a weekly split (full body, upper/lower, PPL) from your goal, experience, equipment and training days
- Picks exercises for your goal (muscle, strength, fat loss, athletic power, general health), focus areas and joints to protect
- Pairs complementary muscles (antagonist supersets, non-competing pairs)
- Guides each session set by set with load suggestions (double progression), rest timer and a checklist
- Tap any exercise for how-to steps, common mistakes, a demo link, your history, a quick weight log and personal notes
- Works offline; data stays on your device (Setup → Download backup to move it)

## Install on your phone
1. Open the site in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android).
2. iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. Android: ⋮ → Install app.
